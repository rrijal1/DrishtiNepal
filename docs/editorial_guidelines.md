# Drishti Nepal — Editorial Guidelines

## Core Principles

1. **Accuracy First**: Never trade speed for accuracy. Verify before publishing.
2. **Source Everything**: Every factual claim must link to a verifiable source.
3. **Political Neutrality**: Track all parties and ministers equally. No editorial bias.
4. **Right of Reply**: Any minister or government office can submit corrections.
5. **Transparency**: AI-generated content is clearly labeled. Methodology is public.
6. **Public Interest**: Every piece of content must serve the public's right to know.

## Content Tiers

### Tier 1: Auto-Publish (No human review needed)

- Factual news summaries from verified sources
- Score updates (system-generated)
- Gazette/official document references
- Criteria: AI confidence > 0.9, purely factual, no opinion

### Tier 2: Quick Review (Content moderator)

- Action classifications (achievement/failure)
- Manifesto item linkages
- AI-generated analysis of routine developments
- Turnaround: within 2 hours

### Tier 3: Full Review (Editor-in-Chief)

- Scholarly articles and deep analysis
- Coverage of controversies or scandals
- Any content that could be perceived as biased
- Turnaround: within 24 hours

### Tier 4: Legal Review

- Content involving corruption allegations
- Anything potentially defamatory
- Content about ongoing legal proceedings
- Turnaround: consult legal counsel before publishing

## AI Content Rules

- All AI-generated text MUST be labeled: "This article was generated with AI assistance and reviewed by Drishti Nepal editors."
- AI should not make subjective judgments — only classify based on defined criteria
- AI confidence scores should be logged but not displayed publicly
- Human editors have final say on all content

## Language Standards

- **Nepali**: Standard written Nepali (मानक नेपाली). Avoid dialectal variations.
- **English**: International English. Clear, concise, professional.
- **Bilingual**: All content published in both languages simultaneously.
- **Proper nouns**: Use official Romanization for Nepali names when available.

## Correction Policy

When errors are found:

1. Correct the content immediately
2. Add a correction notice at the bottom: "Correction (date): Original text stated X. This has been corrected to Y."
3. Log the correction in the audit trail
4. If the error was significant, post a correction notice on social media

## Social Media Guidelines

- Every post links back to the full article on the portal
- No editorializing in social media captions
- Respond to comments professionally; never argue
- Don't engage with trolls; block repeat offenders
- Posting frequency: max 10 posts/day on X, 5 on Facebook
- Optimal times: 7 AM, 9 AM, 12 PM, 5 PM, 8 PM NPT
