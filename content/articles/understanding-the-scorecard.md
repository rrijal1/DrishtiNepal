## The Goal: Bridging Promises and Actions

At Drishti Nepal, our core mission is to bring transparency to the political process. A key part of this is tracking the promises made by political parties in their election manifestos and comparing them to the actual decisions and actions taken by the government.

Our automated scorecard is designed to provide an objective, data-driven measure of alignment between promises and performance.

## How is the Score Calculated?

Each minister and the cabinet as a whole receives a score based on a weighted average of two key factors:

*   **Manifesto Alignment (70%):** Our AI agents analyze government actions, policies, and statements. They then compare these actions to the specific promises laid out in the ruling party's election manifesto. Actions that directly fulfill a promise receive a positive score, while those that contradict it receive a negative score.
*   **Execution & Public Sentiment (30%):** It's not enough to just announce a policy. This component measures the real-world impact. Our system analyzes news reports and public discourse to gauge the sentiment and progress of implemented policies. Positive sentiment and successful project completion contribute positively to this part of the score.

This 70/30 split ensures that the primary focus remains on manifesto commitments, while also acknowledging the importance of effective implementation and public reception.

## A Tool for Voters

This scorecard is not a final judgment, but a tool for you, the citizen. It provides a consistent, unbiased starting point for evaluating the government's performance based on their own stated goals. We encourage you to explore the scores, read the related news, and draw your own informed conclusions.
