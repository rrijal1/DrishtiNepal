## The Goal: Measuring Real Progress

At Drishti Nepal, we track the promises made by political parties in their election manifestos and compare them to measurable outcomes. Our scorecard provides an objective, data-driven measure of whether the government is delivering on its stated commitments.

## How is the Score Calculated?

Each minister receives a score from 0 to 100, based entirely on **outcome indicators** — measurable targets tied to specific manifesto commitments.

For each indicator, we calculate progress as:

- **Higher-is-better** (e.g., GDP growth, school enrollment): how far you've moved from baseline toward target
- **Lower-is-better** (e.g., poverty rate, corruption index): how far you've reduced from baseline toward target

Progress is clamped between 0% and 100%. This means we don't penalize beyond zero, and we cap at the target.

### Minister Scores

A minister's score is a **weighted average** of all outcome indicators assigned to their portfolio. Each indicator carries a weight reflecting its importance. The formula:

> **Minister Score = (sum of weight × progress for each indicator) / (sum of weights) × 100**

### National Score

The national score aggregates all outcome indicators across every portfolio, giving an overall picture of manifesto delivery.

## What We Don't Score

Government actions, gazette entries, and parliament records are displayed alongside each manifesto commitment for context — but they **do not** affect the numeric score. Only verifiable outcome data moves the needle. This keeps the scorecard grounded in facts, not announcements.

## A Tool for Voters

This scorecard is not a final judgment, but a tool for you, the citizen. It provides a consistent, unbiased starting point for evaluating the government's performance based on their own stated goals. We encourage you to explore the scores, read the related evidence, and draw your own informed conclusions.
