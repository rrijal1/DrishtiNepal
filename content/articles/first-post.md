## A New Way to Collaborate

Welcome! We're launching a new section for in-depth, human-written articles and analysis.

This content is written in simple Markdown files and managed directly in our project's GitHub repository. This "Git-as-CMS" approach allows our team and external collaborators to write, review, and publish content in a transparent and efficient way.

### How It Works

1.  **Write in Markdown:** Articles are just `.md` files in the `/content/articles` directory.
2.  **Review via Pull Request:** New articles or edits are submitted as Pull Requests, allowing for peer review.
3.  **Publish Automatically:** Once merged, the site rebuilds automatically to include the new content.

We're excited to bring you more detailed stories and analysis through this new platform.
